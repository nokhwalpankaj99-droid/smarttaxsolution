IIT (BHU) Library Professional Trainee Recruitment 2026–27 – 13 Posts

Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi – Shreenivas Deshpande Library

The Shreenivas Deshpande Library at IIT (BHU) invites applications for 13 one-year Library Professional Trainee assignments. The official notice specifies a consolidated stipend of ₹25,000 per month and an online application deadline of 30 November 2026.

Total posts / assignments13 assignments
LocationVaranasi, Uttar Pradesh, India
Employment typeOne-year temporary traineeship (not regular employment)
Salary / stipendStipend ₹25,000 per month, consolidated; no additional perks
Last date30 November 2026
Notice / referenceVacancy for Library Professional Trainees 2026–27 (17 November 2026)

Deadline: 30 November 2026; closing time not stated in the official notice.

Apply on IIT (BHU) PortalOfficial Notification / PDF

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

First Class in Master of Library and Information Science (M.L.I.Sc.) or equivalent from a recognised Indian university/institute. Only candidates who passed final-year M.L.I.Sc. examinations in 2025 or 2026 should apply. Maximum age is 27 years on the last date; relaxations for OBC, SC, ST and PwBD candidates apply as per Government of India rules. Relevant computer knowledge is desirable.

Post-wise details

Library Professional Trainee – 13 assignments, for one year from the date of joining. Trainees may work in different library sections and shifts, including weekends/holidays on a rotational six-day week.

Salary / stipend

₹25,000 per month, consolidated, with no additional perks. This is a traineeship stipend, not a regular pay scale.

Important dates

Notice dated 17 November 2026. Online application deadline: 30 November 2026. Closing time is not specified.

Selection process

The Institute may conduct a written test and/or interview. The date will be communicated to shortlisted candidates by email or published on the Institute website. Meeting minimum eligibility does not guarantee a call.

How to apply

  1. Read the official PDF notice carefully.
  2. Open the IIT (BHU) Library Professional Trainee application portal.
  3. Complete the online form and pay the ₹500 application fee.
  4. Submit by 30 November 2026 and retain the acknowledgement.
