BARC Medical Officer (Pathology) Recruitment 2026 – Walk-in Interview

Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC), Medical Division, Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India

BARC Medical Division notice for one Medical Officer (Pathology) on adhoc/locum basis for a leave vacancy. Walk-in interview is on 23-11-2026 at 10:30 hrs. Candidates must report at 08:30 hrs for verification; candidates reporting after 10:00 hrs will not be entertained. Appointment is for 178 days, not more than 89 days at one time.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

1 post. MD / DNB in Pathology from a recognised University. Maximum age 50 years on the date of interview.

Post-wise details

Medical Officer (Pathology) – 1 locum / leave-vacancy post; appointment period 178 days, not more than 89 days at one time.

Age limit

Not more than 50 years on the date of interview.

Important dates

Walk-in interview: Monday, 23-11-2026 at 10:30 hrs onwards. Reporting for document verification: 08:30 hrs; candidates reporting after 10:00 hrs will not be entertained.

Selection process

Walk-in interview. If more than 10 candidates respond, candidates will be shortlisted on the basis of the highest percentage obtained in MD / DNB (Pathology).

How to apply

Attend the walk-in interview at BARC Hospital, Anushaktinagar, Mumbai. Report to Conference Room No. 1, Ground Floor, for verification at 08:30 hrs; the interview venue is Conference Room No. 2. Bring the duly filled application format attached to the official notice.

Documents required

Filled application form; SSLC / SSC / HSC certificates; MBBS and MD / DNB passing certificates and marksheets; internship and registration certificates; experience certificates; one set of self-attested copies; one passport-size photograph; and original certificates for verification.
