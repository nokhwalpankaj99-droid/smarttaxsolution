Eligibility

PhD in Wildlife Science. The notified age range is 21–35 years as on the closing date, with applicable Government of India relaxations. Relevant research experience and publications in wildlife habitat assessment using remote sensing/GIS, endangered-species ecology and conservation, or humanities/Indian knowledge systems and biodiversity conservation are desirable as specified in the advertisement.

Wildlife Science में PhD आवश्यक है। आयु सीमा 2 दिसम्बर 2026 को 21–35 वर्ष है; सरकारी नियमों के अनुसार छूट लागू होगी। Wildlife habitat assessment, RS/GIS, endangered species ecology/conservation या biodiversity conservation में शोध अनुभव और publications वांछनीय हैं।
