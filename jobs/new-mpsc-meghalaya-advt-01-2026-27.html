Meghalaya PSC Recruitment 2026 – AE Mechanical, ARO, Coaches & Journalist

Meghalaya Public Service Commission (MPSC), Government of Meghalaya

Meghalaya PSC Advertisement No. 01/2026-2027 dated 12-11-2026 invites online applications for 20 vacancies in PWD (R&B), Finance (Economic Affairs), Sports & Youth Affairs and Information & Public Relations. Online applications close at 17:00 hrs on 12-12-2026.

Total vacancies20 posts: Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) 4; Assistant Research Officer (Finance) 5; Junior Athletic Coach 2; Junior Archery Coach 1; Journalist 8
LocationMeghalaya, India; examination centres include Shillong, Tura, Jowai, Williamnagar and Nongstoin
Employment typeMeghalaya State Government – Direct Recruitment
Salary / PayRevised Pay Structure: Level 15 for Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) and Assistant Research Officer; Level 11 for Junior Athletic / Archery Coaches; Level 8 for Journalist.
Last date2026-12-12
AdvertisementMPSC Advertisement No. 01/2026-2027

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

20 posts across five post types: Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) 4 – B.E. Mechanical Engineering; Assistant Research Officer (Finance) 5 – Master's in Economics/Statistics/Commerce/Mathematics or relevant Honours degree with 5 years' Government Accounting/Banking/Statistics experience; Junior Athletic Coach 2 and Junior Archery Coach 1 – relevant graduate / sports-coaching diploma or B.Sc Sports Coaching route; Journalist 8 – graduate with Diploma in Mass Communication/Journalism or graduate in Mass Communication/Journalism. Age is 21–32 for Assistant Engineer and 18–32 for the other posts, with relaxations as notified.

Post-wise details

Assistant Engineer (Mechanical), PWD (R&B), Group A – 4 posts, Pay Level 15. Assistant Research Officer, Finance (Economic Affairs) – 5 posts, Pay Level 15. Junior Athletic Coach, Directorate of Sports & Youth Affairs – 2 posts, Pay Level 11. Junior Archery Coach, Directorate of Sports & Youth Affairs – 1 post, Pay Level 11. Journalist, Directorate of Information & Public Relations and subordinate offices – 8 posts, Pay Level 8.

Age limit

Assistant Engineer: 21–32 years as on 01-01-2026. Assistant Research Officer and Journalist: 18–32 years as on 01-01-2026. Junior Athletic / Archery Coach: 18–32 years as on 01-01-2026. SC/ST and other relaxations are governed by the advertisement and Meghalaya rules.

Important dates

Advertisement dated: 12-11-2026. Last date for online application: 12-12-2026 at 17:00 hrs.

Selection process

Screening Test / written examination as prescribed for each post, followed by Personal Interview for candidates who qualify. Post-wise screening subjects are specified in the official PDF.

How to apply

Register or log in on the official Meghalaya PSC website, open the Online Application section and submit the application for Advertisement No. 01/2026-2027. Pay the post-specific fee through the notified online gateway or e-Challan before 12-12-2026, 17:00 hrs.

Documents required

Matriculation / SSLC certificate for date of birth; post-specific educational and professional qualifications; experience certificates where required; Meghalaya SC/ST / category / PwBD certificates where applicable; and other certificates prescribed in the advertisement.
