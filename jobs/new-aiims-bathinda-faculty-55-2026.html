AIIMS Bathinda Faculty Recruitment 2026 – 55 Group A Professor Posts

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), Bathinda · Bathinda, Punjab, India

AIIMS Bathinda has opened direct recruitment for 55 Group-A faculty posts across four faculty levels and multiple clinical and pre-clinical departments.

AIIMS Bathinda ने विभिन्न clinical और pre-clinical विभागों में चार faculty levels पर कुल 55 Group-A पदों के लिए direct recruitment शुरू की है।

Vacancies / पद55 posts: 17 Professor, 4 Additional Professor, 16 Associate Professor and 18 Assistant Professor
Last date / अंतिम तिथि30 November 2026, 5:00 PM IST (online form); hard copy by 03 December 2026, 5:00 PM IST
Official Notification AIIMS Bathinda Mandatory Google Form

Age limitMaximum 58 years for Professor/Additional Professor and 50 years for Associate Professor/Assistant Professor as on 30 November 2026; relaxations as per the notice.
Application feeGeneral/OBC/EWS: ₹2,360; SC/ST: ₹1,180; PwBD: Nil (amount includes 18% GST). Separate application and fee are required for each post.

Eligibility / योग्यता

Essential qualification and experience are department- and faculty-level specific and are set out in official Annexure-A. Applicants must meet the applicable medical qualification, registration and post-qualification experience requirements recognized by NMC / Government of India.

आवश्यक qualification और अनुभव विभाग तथा faculty level के अनुसार अलग-अलग हैं और आधिकारिक Annexure-A में दिए गए हैं। उम्मीदवार को संबंधित medical qualification, registration और post-qualification experience की शर्तें पूरी करनी होंगी।

Professor: 17 posts; Additional Professor: 4; Associate Professor: 16; Assistant Professor: 18. Department-wise vacancy matrix and PwBD suitability are in the official 14-page advertisement.

Salary / Pay Scale

7th CPC pay levels: Professor ₹1,68,900–₹2,20,400; Additional Professor ₹1,48,200–₹2,11,400; Associate Professor ₹1,38,300–₹2,09,200; Assistant Professor ₹1,01,500–₹1,67,400, plus admissible allowances/NPA.

7वें वेतन आयोग के अनुसार पद के स्तर पर वेतन: Professor ₹1,68,900–₹2,20,400; Additional Professor ₹1,48,200–₹2,11,400; Associate Professor ₹1,38,300–₹2,09,200; Assistant Professor ₹1,01,500–₹1,67,400, साथ में लागू भत्ते/NPA।

Important Dates / महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ

Online Google Form deadline: 30 November 2026 at 5:00 PM IST. Printed application and supporting documents must reach AIIMS Bathinda by 03 December 2026 at 5:00 PM IST. Interview date will be notified later.

Google Form की अंतिम तिथि 30 नवम्बर 2026, शाम 5 बजे है। भरा हुआ प्रिंटेड आवेदन और दस्तावेज 03 दिसम्बर 2026, शाम 5 बजे तक AIIMS Bathinda पहुँचने चाहिए। Interview की तारीख बाद में घोषित होगी।

Selection Process / चयन प्रक्रिया

Applications are scrutinised and eligible / shortlisted candidates are called for personal interview. If applications are numerous, AIIMS may use an additional shortlisting criterion, which may include a written examination.

आवेदनों की जाँच के बाद पात्र/shortlisted उम्मीदवारों को personal interview के लिए बुलाया जाएगा। आवेदन अधिक होने पर AIIMS अतिरिक्त shortlisting criteria या written examination भी रख सकता है।

How to Apply / आवेदन कैसे करें

Step 1: Submit the mandatory Google Form by 30 November 2026, 5 PM IST. Step 2: Download and complete the prescribed Word application form, pay the fee through SBI Collect, attach the required certificates and send the hard copy by registered/speed post or by hand to the Recruitment Cell, Administrative Block, Medical College Building, Mandi Dabwali Road, AIIMS Bathinda – 151001, Punjab, by 03 December 2026, 5 PM IST.

पहला चरण: 30 नवम्बर 2026, शाम 5 बजे तक अनिवार्य Google Form भरें। दूसरा चरण: निर्धारित Word application form डाउनलोड कर भरें, SBI Collect से शुल्क जमा करें, सभी प्रमाणपत्र लगाएँ और हार्ड कॉपी 03 दिसम्बर 2026, शाम 5 बजे तक Recruitment Cell, Administrative Block, Medical College Building, Mandi Dabwali Road, AIIMS Bathinda – 151001, Punjab भेजें या हाथ से जमा करें।

Candidates applying for more than one post must submit separate applications, separate hard copies and separate fees for each post.

Documents / आवश्यक दस्तावेज

Prescribed application form and candidate profile; mark sheets and degree certificates; FMGE certificate if applicable; Medical Council registration; date-of-birth proof; category/PwBD certificate where applicable; experience certificates; employer NOC where applicable; two passport-size photographs; fee receipt.

निर्धारित आवेदन और profile, mark sheets व degree certificates, लागू होने पर FMGE certificate, Medical Council registration, जन्मतिथि प्रमाण, लागू category/PwBD प्रमाणपत्र, अनुभव प्रमाणपत्र, जहाँ लागू हो employer NOC, दो passport-size फोटो और शुल्क रसीद।
