Indian Air Force AFCAT 01/2027 Recruitment – 284 Specified Vacancies for January 2028 Courses

Indian Air Force

Indian Air Force AFCAT 01/2027 invites applications for Flying and Ground Duty (Technical and Non-Technical) officer entries for courses commencing January 2028. The notification lists 284 specified vacancies across AFCAT and GATE Score entries, plus percentage-based NCC Special Entry seats. Applications close 11 December 2026 at 23:30 IST.

English – Eligibility and recruitment details

Eligibility

Indian citizen and unmarried at commencement of course. Flying Branch: age 20–24 as on 1 January 2028 (valid/current DGCA CPL holders may be up to 26); 10+2 Physics and Mathematics plus a recognised three-year degree or BE/BTech. Ground Duty Technical: age 20–26; 10+2 Physics and Mathematics plus prescribed engineering/technology or equivalent qualification in listed disciplines. Ground Duty Non-Technical: age 20–26; branch-specific graduation/post-graduation qualifications for Weapon Systems, Administration/Logistics, Accounts, Education and Meteorology. NCC Special Entry requires the prescribed Air Wing Senior Division ‘C’ certificate. GATE Score Entry requires eligible engineering degree and qualifying GATE score in specified disciplines. Refer to the official notification for exact subject lists and medical standards.

Vacancy distribution

  • AFCAT Entry – Flying: 36
  • AFCAT Entry – Ground Duty Technical: 124
  • AFCAT Entry – Ground Duty Non-Technical: 108
  • GATE Score Entry – Ground Duty Technical: 16

Total specified vacancies: 284. NCC Special Entry seats are 10% of CDSE vacancies for PC (Men) and 10% of AFCAT vacancies for SSC (Men and Women); the notice does not give a separate fixed count. Vacancies are tentative.

Pay and selection

Flight Cadets receive ₹56,100 per month during training. On commissioning, Flying Officer pay is ₹56,100–₹1,77,500 in Defence Matrix Level 10 plus MSP ₹15,500 and applicable allowances. AFCAT Entry selection includes online written examination, Air Force Selection Board testing, medical examination and merit. NCC and GATE Score entries follow their category-specific routes in the notification.

Important dates and fee

Online applications: 12 November 2026, 11:00 AM to 11 December 2026, 11:30 PM IST. AFCAT examination: 31 January 2027. Courses commence January 2028. AFCAT Entry fee: ₹550 plus 18% GST, non-refundable. Fee is not applicable for NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry.

How to apply

  1. Read the full AFCAT 01/2027 notification and branch-wise eligibility.
  2. Register at the official AFCAT application portal; Aadhaar is mandatory.
  3. Fill personal, qualification and branch-preference details and upload the prescribed photograph, signature and thumb impression.
  4. Pay the applicable fee and submit before 11 December 2026, 11:30 PM IST.
  5. Retain the application PDF/printout and monitor the official portal for updates.
