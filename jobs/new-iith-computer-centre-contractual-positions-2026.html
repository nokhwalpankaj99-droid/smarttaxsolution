IIT Hyderabad Computer Centre Recruitment 2026 – 8 Contractual Technical Posts

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad – Computer Centre

IIT Hyderabad Advertisement IITH/CS/62 invites applications for eight contractual Computer Centre positions: HPC System Engineer (2), Network Engineer (3), Web Developer (1), System Engineer (1) and AV Technician (1). Monthly remuneration varies by role from ₹30,000 to ₹80,000; the initial engagement is 11 months.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Applicants must be Indian nationals. Role-wise qualifications and experience apply; the advertisement specifies age limits of 40 years for HPC System Engineer and 35 years for the other four roles, subject to Government of India relaxations. See the role-wise summary below and the full PDF for all technical requirements.

Post-wise details

  • HPC System Engineer – 2 posts: relevant B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc or M.E./M.Tech/MCA in CS/IT/Information Science/Software Engineering/AI/Data Science; Linux administration, Slurm/PBS or other schedulers, GPU, storage, containers and scripting. ₹35,000–₹80,000/month.
  • Network Engineer – 3 posts: relevant B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech/MCA/M.Sc in CS/IT/ECE/Electrical or related fields; LAN, Wi-Fi, switching, VLAN and fibre networking. ₹30,000–₹45,000/month.
  • Web Developer – 1 post: relevant bachelor’s/master’s degree plus experience developing live websites; Jekyll, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Python, Linux and Git. ₹30,000–₹40,000/month.
  • System Engineer – 1 post: relevant CS/IT/Software/AI/Data Science qualification; Windows/Linux administration, scripting, networking, backup and endpoint support. ₹30,000–₹40,000/month.
  • AV Technician – 1 post: relevant bachelor’s degree with at least one year of experience, or relevant diploma/ITI/NTC with at least two years; AV systems, streaming and conferencing. ₹32,000/month.

Salary / stipend

Monthly consolidated remuneration: HPC System Engineer ₹35,000–₹80,000; Network Engineer ₹30,000–₹45,000; Web Developer ₹30,000–₹40,000; System Engineer ₹30,000–₹40,000; AV Technician ₹32,000.

Important dates

Applications opened on 10 November 2026. Last date: 1 December 2026. No closing time is stated.

Selection process

Written Test, Skill Test and/or Interview, as applicable to the post. IIT Hyderabad may shortlist candidates based on the relevance and level of qualifications and experience.
