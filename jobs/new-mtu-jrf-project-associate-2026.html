Eligibility

M.E./M.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering or equivalent; or B.E./B.Tech in ECE or equivalent; or M.Sc. in Electronics/Physics, with minimum 60% aggregate or 6.5 CGPA at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels. GATE or CSIR-UGC NET is desirable. Maximum age 30 years.
