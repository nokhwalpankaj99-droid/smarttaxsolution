MEA Internship Programme in International Law 2026

The Ministry of External Affairs invites applications for its 2026 International Law Internship Programme. The official timeline sets 25 November 2026 as the last date for applications.

EmployerMinistry of External Affairs, Government of India
PostsMaximum 6 interns for the annual programme
LocationMinistry of External Affairs Headquarters, New Delhi, India
Employment typeInternship (minimum 1 month, maximum 3 months)
Salary / मानदेयHonorarium ₹10,000 per month; one-time travel support subject to the stated airfare ceiling
Last date / अंतिम तिथिLast date for receipt of applications: 25 November 2026. No closing time is stated in the official timeline.

English summary

MEA's International Law Internship offers up to six interns exposure to international relations, diplomacy and international law at the Ministry's headquarters.

Advertisement: MEA Internship Programme in International Law 2026

हिंदी में जानकारी

MEA का International Law Internship Programme नई दिल्ली में अंतरराष्ट्रीय कानून, कूटनीति और विदेश नीति के कार्य का अनुभव देता है। वार्षिक अधिकतम intake 6 interns है।

अंतिम तिथि: Last date for receipt of applications: 25 November 2026. No closing time is stated in the official timeline.

Eligibility and age limit | पात्रता एवं आयु सीमा

Open to Indian citizens with a graduate/postgraduate degree in Law from a recognised university, or students in the final semester/year of an undergraduate/postgraduate law programme where internship is mandatory. Age must not exceed 27 years as on 31 December 2026.

Age / आयु: Not above 27 years as on 31-12-2026.

Salary / remuneration | वेतन / मानदेय

Honorarium ₹10,000 per month. One-time to-and-fro air travel support is provided subject to the prevailing economy-class airfare ceiling between the State capital and Delhi. Interns bear their own boarding and lodging in Delhi.

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Fully online process comprising preliminary screening and personal interview.

How to apply | आवेदन कैसे करें

Register on the official MEA internship portal, review the International Law Internship Policy and submit the online application by 25 November 2026.

Documents: Keep proof of Indian citizenship, law qualification/final-year status, date of birth and academic records ready for online scrutiny and interview.

Important dates | महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ

Notification: 10 November 2026. Application deadline: 25 November 2026. Shortlist/interview invitations: 30 November 2026. Online interviews: 07 December 2026. Selected candidates announced: 14 December 2026.
