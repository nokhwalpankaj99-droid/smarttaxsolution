IIT Hyderabad Project Accountant Recruitment 2026 – WIN Centre

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad – Wadhwani Innovation Network Centre of Excellence

IIT Hyderabad Advertisement IITH/CS/63 invites applications for one contractual Project Accountant at the Wadhwani Innovation Network Centre of Excellence. Consolidated remuneration is ₹40,000–₹45,000 per month; the initial term is 11 months and the deadline is 17 December 2026.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

B.Com from a recognised university/institute; proficiency in Tally/ERP or similar accounting software, MS Office (especially Excel) and financial reporting; and at least three years of post-qualification experience in accounting, financial management, project accounting, project funds, budget monitoring, utilisation certificates and financial documentation. M.Com/MBA Finance/CA Inter/ICWA Inter or similar professional qualifications and experience in sponsored projects are desirable.

Post-wise details

Project Accountant – 1 unreserved post at the Wadhwani Innovation Network Centre of Excellence. Initial appointment: 11 months, with possible extension subject to performance and project requirements.

Salary / stipend

₹40,000–₹45,000 per month, consolidated.

Important dates

Online application commenced: 17 November 2026. Last date: 17 December 2026. No closing time is stated.

Selection process

Written Test / Skill Test / Interview.

How to apply

  1. Read Advertisement IITH/CS/63 and its application instructions.
  2. Open the official recruitment link for Project Accountant (job ID 62).
  3. Complete the online application and upload educational, experience and identity documents by 17 December 2026.
