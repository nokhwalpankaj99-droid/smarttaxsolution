Eligibility & experience | योग्यता और अनुभव

Trade/discipline-specific qualifications include 10th pass for Library Assistant; relevant ITI for designated trades; 12th or ITI for Fire Safety Technician; B.Com for Executive Finance; B.Sc Chemistry for Lab Chemist/Analyst; BBA for Executive HR; any graduate for Secretarial Office Assistant; relevant Diploma for specified technical executive roles; and relevant graduation for NATS graduate apprentices. District/domicile eligibility applies by work centre. See official notification's qualification table.

Salary / pay | वेतन

Stipend: ₹8,200–₹12,300 per month depending on apprentice category and qualification; 12-month apprenticeship.

Post-wise details | पद-वार विवरण

3,047 tentative seats across 24 ONGC work centres and Northern, Mumbai, Western, Southern and Central sectors. Trades include ITI trades, COPA, Secretarial Office Assistant, Accounts Executive, Graduate / Diploma Engineering Executive, Lab Chemist/Analyst and related disciplines. Candidates may select one eligible work-centre/trade; domicile or qualification district restrictions apply. Refer to the official 20-page notification and annexure for the exact work-centre-wise seat matrix.

Important dates | महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ

Advertisement / application opening: 29-10-2026. Last date: 20-11-2026. Result / selection: 15-12-2026. Training duration: 12 months.

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Applications are consolidated and scrutinised trade-wise, qualification-wise and work-centre-wise. Selection is based on eligibility and scrutiny; shortlisted/selected candidates are informed by registered email and results are published on ONGC's official apprenticeship website. Document verification and eligibility compliance are mandatory.

How to apply | आवेदन कैसे करें

Apply through the appropriate official apprenticeship portal: NAPS / Apprenticeship India for designated trade apprentices and NATS for graduate/diploma apprentices. Register or log in, search ONGC apprenticeship engagement, choose an eligible work centre and trade, complete the application and submit it before 20-11-2026. Follow the official ONGC portal instructions and keep the registration/application acknowledgement.

Documents required | आवश्यक दस्तावेज

Class X certificate/marksheet for date of birth; relevant ITI/NCVT/SCVT certificate and marksheets, Diploma or Graduation certificate/marksheets as applicable; domicile / district eligibility proof; category certificate for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/PwBD where applicable; Aadhaar and bank details; and other documents requested on NAPS/NATS or by ONGC during verification.
