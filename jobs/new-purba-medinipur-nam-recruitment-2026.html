Eligibility and age limit | पात्रता एवं आयु सीमा

MPW: graduate from a recognised university and at least one-year computer diploma from a government-registered institution; West Bengal residence and local-language requirements apply. Yoga Instructor: Madhyamik/Secondary pass with yoga certificate, diploma or degree affiliated to the West Bengal Council of Yoga and Naturopathy (WBCYN), WBCYN registration and permanent West Bengal residence. Age is post-specific.

Age / आयु: MPW: 21–40 years; Yoga Instructor: 18–40 years, as on 01-01-2026, with applicable government relaxation.

Salary / remuneration | वेतन / मानदेय

MPW: ₹15,000/month. Yoga Instructor (male): ₹8,000/month (₹250 per session, maximum 32 sessions/month). Yoga Instructor (female): ₹5,000/month (₹250 per session, maximum 20 sessions/month).

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

MPW: academic score, computer test (minimum 50% qualifying) and experience. Yoga Instructor: academic score, demonstration and interview.

How to apply | आवेदन कैसे करें

Apply online through the West Bengal Health Department application portal. Registration and fee payment close on 03 December 2026; complete the full application by midnight on 04 December 2026.

Application fee: ₹100 for General candidates and ₹50 for reserved-category candidates.

Documents: Keep proof of age, educational qualifications, computer diploma (MPW), WBCYN qualification and registration (Yoga Instructor), residence and category certificates, photograph and signature ready.

Important dates | महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ

Notice dated 05 November 2026. Online application: 07 November to midnight 04 December 2026. Registration and fee payment close 03 December 2026.
