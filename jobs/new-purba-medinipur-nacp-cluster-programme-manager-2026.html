Eligibility and age limit | पात्रता एवं आयु सीमा

Candidates must be permanent residents of West Bengal and know the local language. Qualification pathways include a Bachelor's degree in Medical or Allied Health Sciences, or a Master's in Public Health, Healthcare Management/Administration, Social Science, Psychology, Applied Epidemiology, Demography, Statistics, Population Sciences or similar fields. Experience: 3 years for specified Master's pathways; 5 years for specified Bachelor's/other Master's pathways, including at least 2 years in HIV/AIDS for the latter route. Experience is counted after the essential qualification.

Age / आयु: Maximum 50 years as on the closing date of application.

Salary / remuneration | वेतन / मानदेय

₹54,300 per month

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Written test: 50 marks; experience: 20 marks; physical interview including computer test: 30 marks.

How to apply | आवेदन कैसे करें

Apply online through the West Bengal Health Department portal. Complete registration and fee payment by 25 November 2026, then submit the full application by midnight on 26 November 2026.

Application fee: ₹100 for General candidates and ₹50 for reserved-category candidates; non-refundable.

Documents: Keep proof of age, degree, post-qualification experience certificates, West Bengal residence, local-language and category documents (where applicable), photograph and signature ready. Retain the registration printout.
