IIT Bhubaneswar DAY-NRLM Incubator Recruitment 2026 – Project Lead & Business Manager

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhubaneswar, Research and Entrepreneurship Park – DAY-NRLM Incubator Programme

IIT Bhubaneswar’s DAY-NRLM Incubator Programme invites applications for one Project Lead and two Business Manager posts. The Google Form deadline is 26 November 2026 at 11:59 PM.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

3 posts: Project Lead 1 and Business Manager 2. Postgraduate qualification in Business Management, Development Studies, Rural Management, Accounts, Economics or equivalent; CA / ICWA may be considered as specified. Project Lead requires about 12 years’ relevant experience; Business Manager requires at least three years’ experience in enterprise promotion / management.

Post-wise details

Project Lead – 1 post, up to ₹10 lakh/year; Business Manager – 2 posts, up to ₹12 lakh/year, subject to programme terms.

Not stated in the public notice summary; see official PDF.

Shortlisting and selection as prescribed by IIT Bhubaneswar Research and Entrepreneurship Park / DAY-NRLM programme.
