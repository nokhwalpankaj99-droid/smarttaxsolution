CSIR-HRDG Jigyasa Project Personnel Recruitment 2026 – 5 Walk-in Posts

CSIR-HRDG invites Indian nationals for 5 temporary project-personnel posts under the Jigyasa project. Walk-in interview is on 30 November 2026; candidates must report at CSIR-HRDG PUSA between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM.

English summary

CSIR-HRDG has 5 temporary contractual project-personnel posts under the Jigyasa project: Project Scientist-II (1), Project Scientist-I (1), Principal Project Associate (1) and Senior Project Associate (2).

Advertisement: HRDG/JIGYASA/01/2026 dated 11 November 2026; project HCP002601.

General conditions | सामान्य शर्तें

Only Indian nationals may apply. The engagement is temporary and contractual, initially up to 31 March 2027 and co-terminus with the project, subject to review; the project runs up to 31 March 2031. Age relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST/PwD/Women and 3 years for OBC. Final-semester and result-awaited candidates are not eligible where the essential qualification is required. Relevant experience is counted after acquiring the essential qualification.

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Selection is based on academic records and/or interview. Candidates must report at the CSIR-HRDG PUSA Reception Desk between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM on 30 November 2026; late arrivals will not be entertained. In case of a large number of candidates, the interview may continue the following day.

How to attend | आवेदन / walk-in प्रक्रिया

Attend in person at CSIR-HRDG, CSIR Complex, Library Avenue, PUSA, New Delhi-110012. Bring the prescribed filled application form, one latest passport-size photograph, self-attested copies of educational certificates, age proof, category certificate where applicable, experience certificate and detailed biodata. Bring originals for verification.
