IIT Hyderabad Postdoctoral Research Fellow Recruitment 2026 – AI-Driven Graphene Synthesis

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad – CLEANZ Centre of Excellence

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Fresh Ph.D. in Materials Science, Metallurgical, Chemical or Mechanical Engineering, or AI/Computer Science with relevant AI/ML or data-driven materials research. B.E./B.Tech or equivalent in Materials Science, Metallurgical, Chemical or Mechanical Engineering and at least three Scopus-indexed publications. ML, Bayesian optimization, materials informatics, graphene/2D materials and characterization experience are desirable.

Position and fellowship

Postdoctoral Research Fellow for AI-driven scale-up of graphene synthesis and process optimization. One-year term, extension subject to performance review. ₹58,000–₹78,000/month plus 30% HRA if applicable. Post count is not stated.

Dates and selection

Shortlisting on eligibility, academic record and experience; shortlisted candidates receive email about interview. Deadline 25 November 2026, 5:00 PM IST. Position is on-site at IIT Hyderabad.

How to apply

Complete the Google Form linked in the official PDF before 5:00 PM IST on 25 November 2026.
