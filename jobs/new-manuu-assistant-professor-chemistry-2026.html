MANUU Assistant Professor (Contractual) – Chemistry, School of Sciences

Maulana Azad National Urdu University (MANUU), Central University, Ministry of Education, Government of India

MANUU notice dated 16-11-2026 invites candidates for two contractual Assistant Professor posts in Chemistry, School of Sciences. Walk-in interview is on Wednesday, 25-11-2026 at 10:00 a.m. at the University Guest House, MANUU Campus, Gachibowli, Hyderabad. The engagement is temporary and short-term.

LocationWalk-in at MANUU Campus, Gachibowli, Hyderabad, Telangana; selected candidates may be posted at MANUU Headquarters or another University institution in India
Employment typeShort-term contractual Assistant Professor – Walk-in Interview
Salary / PaySalary not exceeding ₹50,000 per month as per UGC norms.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

2 Chemistry posts. Master's degree with at least 55% marks in the concerned / relevant / allied subject, plus UGC NET / CSIR NET / SLET / SET or a qualifying Ph.D. exemption under UGC Regulations. Knowledge of Urdu is compulsory under the University's stated policy; retired teachers below 65 years may also be eligible under the notice.

Post-wise details

Assistant Professor (Contractual), Chemistry, School of Sciences – 2 posts. Reservation categories are as per Government of India rules.

Age limit

No general upper age limit is specified in the notice. Retired teachers from recognised institutions below 65 years are also stated to be eligible.

Important dates

Walk-in interview: Wednesday, 25-11-2026. Reporting time: 10:00 a.m.

Selection process

Walk-in interview by the University.

How to apply

Attend the walk-in interview at the Committee Room, University Guest House, MANUU Campus, Gachibowli, Hyderabad. Candidates must attend at their own expense and bring the required originals and attested copies.

Documents required

Resume; original educational and experience certificates; proof of Urdu knowledge; testimonials; passport-size photograph; and one set of attested photocopies for verification.
