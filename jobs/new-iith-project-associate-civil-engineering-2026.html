IIT Hyderabad Project Associate Recruitment 2026 – Civil Engineering Project

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad – Department of Civil Engineering

IIT Hyderabad invites applications for one temporary Project Associate position in the Consultancy Group Housing Project under Civil Engineering. The official notice specifies ₹60,000 per month including HRA, an initial 11-month appointment and a 5 December 2026 deadline.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

B.Com with a relevant postgraduate qualification; Higher Grade English Typing Certificate; Diploma in Computer Programming from a recognised institution; and 10 years of relevant experience in projects, project coordination or related activities involving construction and maintenance. Consultancy experience, particularly at academic institutions, is preferred.

Post-wise details

Project Associate – 1 position for the Consultancy Group Housing Project, Department of Civil Engineering. Initial appointment: 11 months; extension may depend on project requirements, performance and funding.

Salary / stipend

₹60,000 per month, including HRA.

Important dates

IIT Hyderabad Careers listing date: 12 November 2026. Last date to apply: 5 December 2026. No closing time is stated.

Selection process

Candidates will be shortlisted on academic qualifications, technical qualifications and relevant experience. Only shortlisted candidates will be informed by email regarding an interview with the selection committee.

How to apply

  1. Read the official Project Associate PDF.
  2. Open the IIT Hyderabad Careers page and select the Project Associate temporary-position notice to access the prescribed application form.
  3. Submit the online form by 5 December 2026.
