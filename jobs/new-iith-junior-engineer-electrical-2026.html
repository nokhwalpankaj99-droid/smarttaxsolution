IIT Hyderabad Junior Engineer (Electrical) Recruitment 2026 – 1 Post

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad – Electrical Services / Construction and Maintenance

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Either B.E./B.Tech in Electrical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering or Electronics and Communication Engineering with at least two years of relevant post-qualification experience; OR a relevant diploma in these branches with at least three years of experience. Relevant work includes electrical power distribution, substation O&M/erection, building electrification, HVAC maintenance, SCADA/BMS automation and related electrical maintenance. Computer literacy is required.

Post-wise details

Junior Engineer (Electrical) – 1 unreserved post. Initial contract period: six months, with possible extension subject to satisfactory performance and institute requirements.

₹50,000 per month consolidated, including ₹5,000 transportation allowance.

Important dates

Selection process

Written Test / Skill Test / Interview.
