Selection process / चयन प्रक्रिया

Administrative Officer / Accounts Officer / Purchase & Stores Officer: CBT (Part A general paper + Part B curriculum-based paper) followed by Personal Interview; CBT and interview weightage 60:40. Junior Personal Assistant: CBT followed by qualifying stenography skill test (10-minute dictation at 80 w.p.m.; transcription 50 minutes English / 65 minutes Hindi). Technical Assistant and Technician B: CBT (Part A general paper + Part B trade/discipline paper) followed by qualifying trade skill test; final merit is based on CBT marks among skill-test qualifiers. Exact qualifying marks and shortlisting rules are in the official notification.

Exam pattern / परीक्षा पैटर्न

Part A common paper: General English (25), Quantitative Aptitude (25), General Intelligence & Logical Ability (25), General Knowledge / Current Affairs (25). For Officer, Technical Assistant and Technician B, Part B is a 100-question curriculum / discipline / trade paper. Officer CBT duration: 120 minutes (Part A 60 + Part B 60); JPA CBT: 100 questions in 60 minutes; Technical Assistant / Technician B CBT: 200 questions in 120 minutes. One mark per correct answer and 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer. Post-specific Part B syllabus is in Section 12 of the 51-page notification.
