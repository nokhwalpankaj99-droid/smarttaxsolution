English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Bachelor’s, Master’s or Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or a related field. Applicants should have strong experience in deep-learning models using PyTorch or TensorFlow; image-processing techniques with OpenCV, scikit-image or SimpleITK; Python and C++ and/or MATLAB; and familiarity with Git, Docker and Linux. Ultrasound physics, medical-imaging modalities and toolkits such as MONAI/ITK are preferred.
