IIT (BHU) Junior Research Assistant Recruitment 2026 – Pharmaceutical Engineering

Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi – Department of Pharmaceutical Engineering & Technology

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Indian citizen and Uttar Pradesh resident. M.Sc. in Computer Science, IT, Data Science or AI/ML; M.Pharm; B.E./B.Tech; or equivalent. First division in graduation and post-graduation is required under CSTUP criteria. Maximum age 28 years as on 1 April of the application year; no relaxation under the prescribed CSTUP eligibility.

Post and pay

One Junior Research Assistant post for the Innovate Form machine-learning platform project. Fellowship: ₹25,000 per month for the first two years and ₹28,000 per month in the third year. Initial appointment is one year and may be extended up to three years.

Dates, selection and application

Listed on IIT (BHU) positions page on 20 November 2026. Complete application must reach the Principal Investigator by 8 December 2026. Shortlisting is by the PI; shortlisted candidates are contacted by email for interview.

  1. Download the prescribed form.
  2. Attach CV and self-attested education, date-of-birth, UP residence and experience documents.
  3. Email to rchawla.phe@itbhu.ac.in with subject “Application for Junior Research Assistant (JRA) – CSTUP Project 8785”.

पात्रता

भारतीय नागरिक और उत्तर प्रदेश निवासी होना आवश्यक है। M.Sc. (CS/IT/Data Science/AI-ML), M.Pharm, B.E./B.Tech या समकक्ष योग्यता तथा Graduation और Post-Graduation दोनों में प्रथम श्रेणी चाहिए। अधिकतम आयु 28 वर्ष (1 अप्रैल के अनुसार); निर्धारित CSTUP पात्रता में छूट नहीं बताई गई है।

पद और fellowship

Innovate Form project में एक Junior Research Assistant पद। पहले दो वर्षों में ₹25,000 और तीसरे वर्ष ₹28,000 प्रति माह fellowship। प्रारंभिक नियुक्ति एक वर्ष की है, जिसे तीन वर्ष तक बढ़ाया जा सकता है।
