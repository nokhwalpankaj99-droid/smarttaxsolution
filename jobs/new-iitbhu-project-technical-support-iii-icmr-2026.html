English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Relevant M.Pharm, M.S. (Pharm.), M.Tech (Pharm.), M.Tech in Medicinal Chemistry or Pharmaceutical Chemistry. The official PDF is inconsistent: the opening paragraph refers to second-class PG with Ph.D., while the detailed essential-qualification table says second-class PG with one year industrial/research experience. First-class qualifications are also listed. Maximum age 32; GoI relaxations apply. Confirm the applicable condition with the PI.

Post and pay

One Project Technical Support-III post under ICMR project EMDR/SG/14/2025-06465 on PROTACs for head-and-neck cancer research. Initial one-year appointment; ₹28,000/month plus 20% HRA.

Dates, selection and application

The PDF expressly states 2 December 2026 as the last date. Shortlisted applicants are emailed for interview; no TA/DA.

Use the attached form and self-attested certificates. Email Rajnish.phe@iitbhu.ac.in or send registered post to the Department of Pharmaceutical Engineering & Technology, IIT (BHU), Varanasi. Subject: “Application for Project Technical Support (III) in ICMR-funded project”.
