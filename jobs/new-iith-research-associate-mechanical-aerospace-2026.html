IIT Hyderabad Research Associate Recruitment 2026 – Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad – Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering

IIT Hyderabad invites applications for one Research Associate under a project on modelling process parameters for PCS fibres and the PCS–SiC conversion process. The fellowship is ₹58,000 per month plus 27% HRA if applicable; applications close on 30 November 2026.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Ph.D. in Solid Mechanics, Applied Mathematics, Applied Mechanics, Mechanical Engineering or a related field, with prior experience in continuum mechanics. Candidates who have submitted their Ph.D. thesis are encouraged to apply. Strong interest in mathematics and continuum mechanics, and experience in one-dimensional modelling/simulation using nonlinear theories, are desirable.

Post-wise details

Research Associate – one position in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering for the advertised PCS-fibre / PCS–SiC modelling project.

Salary / stipend

Research fellowship ₹58,000 per month plus 27% HRA, if applicable.

Important dates

IIT Hyderabad Careers listing date: 20 November 2026. Last date to apply: 30 November 2026. No closing time is stated.

Selection process

Applications will be shortlisted on academic record and achievements. Only shortlisted candidates will be informed by email for an online interview with the selection committee.

How to apply

  1. Read the official advertisement PDF.

  3. Submit by 30 November 2026 and monitor the registered email for any interview communication.
