Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Merit list based on the simple average of percentage marks in Class X (minimum 50% aggregate) and ITI marks in the relevant trade. No centralized merit list; document verification is conducted by the opted Division/Unit.

How to apply | आवेदन कैसे करें

Apply online only at the official RRC Central Railway website rrccr.com. Register, select the relevant unit/cluster and trade, enter Class X and ITI marks, upload required documents, pay the applicable fee and submit by 02-12-2026, 5:00 PM. No hard copy is to be sent.

Documents required | आवश्यक दस्तावेज

Class X/SSC marksheet and date-of-birth proof; consolidated ITI marksheet; NCVT/SCVT National Trade Certificate or provisional certificate; SC/ST/OBC-NCL certificate where applicable; PwBD certificate where applicable; discharge/serving certificate for Ex-Servicemen/Armed Forces quota; and originals for document verification.
