Eligibility | पात्रता

Only departmental employees of Kerala State Electricity Board Ltd. Age 18–50 (DOB 02-01-1976 to 01-01-2008 inclusive). Selection through a four-paper qualifying OMR examination; candidates must meet service eligibility and submit a service certificate.

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Qualifying and competitive OMR examination of four papers, each 100 marks and 75 minutes: Financial & Cost Accounting; Corporate Laws; Direct and Indirect Taxes; Electricity Act, Kerala Electricity Supply Code, Regulations & PWD Code. Minimum 35 marks in each paper and 160/400 aggregate. One-third mark is deducted for each wrong answer. Successful candidates undergo 9 months of comprehensive training.
