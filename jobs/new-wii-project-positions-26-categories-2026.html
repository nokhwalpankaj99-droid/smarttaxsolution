Salary and selection

Salary: Post-wise monthly emoluments vary by category; the official notice lists amounts from ₹24,000 to ₹1,25,000 plus HRA for different roles.

Selection process: Screening is based on the criteria for each category, including qualification marks, specialization, experience, publications and NET/CSIR/ICAR credentials where applicable. The top ten shortlisted candidates for each advertised position are called for interview.

वेतन और चयन प्रक्रिया

वेतन: पद-वार मानदेय अलग है; विज्ञापन में अलग-अलग भूमिकाओं के लिए ₹24,000 से ₹1,25,000 प्रतिमाह तक तथा HRA का उल्लेख है।

चयन: प्रत्येक पद के लिए योग्यता, अंक, specialization, experience और publications के आधार पर screening होगी; हर पद के लिए अधिकतम 10 shortlisted उम्मीदवार interview के लिए बुलाए जाएँगे।

Online application opens 17 November 2026 and closes 1 December 2026. The notice does not state a closing time.

Application fee: General category: ₹500. SC/ST/OBC/EWS and physically challenged applicants are exempt from the application fee but must pay ₹100 processing fee.

महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ और शुल्क

ऑनलाइन आवेदन 17 नवम्बर 2026 को खुले और 1 दिसम्बर 2026 को बंद होंगे; closing time नोटिस में नहीं दिया गया।

शुल्क: General category: ₹500. SC/ST/OBC/EWS and physically challenged applicants are exempt from the application fee but must pay ₹100 processing fee.

How to apply

Apply online at the WII Recruitment and Placement Cell portal. A candidate may apply for a maximum of two positions. Upload scanned qualification, marksheet, experience, category, NET/publication documents, photograph, signature and fee receipt as applicable.

Documents: Educational certificates and marksheets, work-experience proof, category certificate where applicable, UGC/CSIR/ICAR NET or GATE proof where relevant, publications, passport-size photo, digital signature and processing-fee receipt.

आवेदन कैसे करें

WII-RP Cell के आधिकारिक portal पर अधिकतम दो पदों के लिए आवेदन करें। प्रमाणपत्र, marksheets, अनुभव, NET/GATE, publications, photo, signature और fee receipt upload करें।

दस्तावेज़: शैक्षणिक प्रमाणपत्र, अंकतालिकाएँ, अनुभव प्रमाणपत्र, आयु/पहचान प्रमाण और जहाँ लागू हो category certificate तैयार रखें।
