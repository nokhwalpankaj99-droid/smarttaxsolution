Bureau of Energy Efficiency Recruitment 2026 – 23 Deputation Posts

Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India

BEE has extended the closing date for its 23 deputation vacancies across six technical and non-technical posts to 23 November 2026. Applications are to be forwarded through proper channel to the Secretary, BEE, New Delhi.

Total vacancies23 posts
LocationBEE, Sewa Bhawan, R.K. Puram, New Delhi, Delhi, India
Employment typeDeputation – Group A
Salary / PayPay Levels 10, 11 and 12 (7th CPC; post-wise pay matrix in notification)
Last date2026-11-23
AdvertisementBEE deputation vacancy circular; Employment News 10–16 October 2026

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

23 posts: Joint Director (Technical) 8; Joint Director (Non-Technical) 1; Deputy Director (Technical) 11; Deputy Director (Non-Technical) 1; Assistant Director (HR) 1; Assistant Director (Finance) 1. Deputation is open to eligible officers of Central/State/UT Governments, universities, recognised research institutes, PSUs and autonomous/statutory bodies. Post-wise requirements include analogous posts / specified regular service in feeder pay levels, relevant engineering/science or HR/Finance qualifications, Energy Auditor/Manager credentials for technical roles and prescribed experience. Maximum age 56 years.

Post-wise details

Joint Director (Technical) – 8, Level 12; Joint Director (Non-Technical) – 1, Level 12; Deputy Director (Technical) – 11, Level 11; Deputy Director (Non-Technical) – 1, Level 11; Assistant Director (HR) – 1, Level 10; Assistant Director (Finance) – 1, Level 10. All posts are in Delhi.

Age limit

Maximum 56 years as on the closing date, subject to post-specific rules.

Important dates

Extended last date: 23-11-2026. The extension notice does not state a closing time.

Selection process

Deputation selection after scrutiny of cadre, pay-level, qualification, experience and service records.

How to apply

Send Annexure-I proforma through proper channel to the Secretary, Bureau of Energy Efficiency, Ministry of Power, 4th Floor, Sewa Bhawan, R.K. Puram, New Delhi–110066. Attach two passport-size photographs, complete ACR/APARs for the last five years, vigilance clearance and major/minor penalty statement for the last ten years.

Documents required

Annexure-I CV proforma; two passport-size photographs; ACR/APARs for five years; vigilance clearance; penalty statement for ten years; qualification and service records.
