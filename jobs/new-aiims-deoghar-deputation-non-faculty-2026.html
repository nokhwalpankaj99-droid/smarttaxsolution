English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Deputation-only. Serving officers of Central/State/UT Governments, universities, statutory/autonomous bodies or R&D organisations holding analogous regular posts or meeting post-specific regular-service requirements. Engineering, nursing, administration, accounts, pharmacy and clerical posts have different qualifications. Maximum age 56 years.

Posts and pay levels

  • Executive Engineer (Civil) 1, Level 11
  • Executive Engineer (Electrical) 1, Level 11
  • Nursing Superintendent 2, Level 11
  • Deputy Nursing Superintendent 2, Level 10
  • Assistant Administrative Officer 2, Level 7
  • Assistant Accounts Officer 3, Level 7
  • Chief Pharmacist 1, Level 7
  • Junior Administrative Officer 1, Level 6
  • Upper Division Clerk 4, Level 4

Dates and selection

Advertisement dated 18 November 2026. Proper-channel forwarding within 30 days of Employment News publication; receipt at AIIMS within 45 days. Calendar date not printed. Selection committee evaluates service records and past performance; interview may be held.

How to apply

Complete Annexure-I/II, attach five years’ APARs, vigilance/integrity certificates, penalty statement, qualification/experience and cadre clearance. Send by Speed Post/Registered Post through proper channel to Deputy Director (Administration), AIIMS Deoghar, Devipur Campus, Ramsagar, Deoghar-814117, Jharkhand. Separate application per post.
