Eligibility

10th class pass (or equivalent under the 10+2 system) with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognised board, plus a National Trade Certificate in the notified trade issued by NCVT/SCVT. Trade-wise eligibility and vacancy position are in the revised detailed notification.

How to apply

Documents: 10th marksheet, ITI National Trade Certificate, scanned passport photograph, signature, valid email ID and mobile number, and category proof where applicable.
