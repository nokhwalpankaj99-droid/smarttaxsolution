ACTREC Scientific Assistant Recruitment 2026 – Outsourced Contract

Advanced Centre for Treatment, Research and Education in Cancer (ACTREC), Tata Memorial Centre, Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India; outsourced contractor A-1 Facility and Property Managers Pvt. Ltd.

ACTREC outsourced notice OS-A/CCE/022/2026 dated 17-11-2026 invites candidates for one Scientific Assistant post at the Centre for Cancer Epidemiology. Walk-in interview is on 25-11-2026 at Room No. 205, 2nd Floor, CCE, TMC-ACTREC, Sector 22, Kharghar, Navi Mumbai. Reporting time is 10:00 AM to 10:30 AM. Contract is for six months and may be extended as required.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

1 post. M.Tech / M.Sc. Biotechnology, or M.Sc. Life Sciences (Botany, Zoology, Biochemistry, Applied Biology or Microbiology); preferably one year of experience. Desirable skills include molecular biology / laboratory techniques, biological sample handling, DNA extraction, PCR, genotyping, sequencing-library preparation, blood separation / fractionation, mass spectrometry and chromatography. Age up to 35 years, may be relaxed on experience basis.
