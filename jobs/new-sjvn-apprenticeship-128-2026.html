Eligibility

Only Himachal Pradesh domicile candidates. Graduate: full-time 4-year B.E./B.Tech in the relevant engineering branch; Diploma: full-time 3-year engineering polytechnic diploma; ITI: Matriculation plus regular full-time NCVT/SCVT ITI in the relevant trade. Age 18–30 years as on 17-12-2026. Candidates with one year or more job/training experience after education, or prior SJVN apprenticeship, are not eligible.
