NEIGRIHMS IRDL Recruitment 2026 – Research Assistant

North Eastern Indira Gandhi Regional Institute of Health and Medical Sciences (NEIGRIHMS), Department of Microbiology, Shillong

NEIGRIHMS Department of Microbiology invites candidates for two Research Assistant posts under the DHR-ICMR-funded IRDL project. Walk-in document verification and interview are scheduled for 27-11-2026 at 09:30 AM in the Seminar Room, Department of Microbiology, NEIGRIHMS, Shillong.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

2 posts. M.Sc. MLT / M.Sc. in Life Sciences / Molecular Biology from a recognised University or Institute. Preference for two years' Microbiology / Molecular Biology experience, computer / data-management skills and peer-reviewed publications. Maximum age 30 years, relaxations as per rules.

Post-wise details

Research Assistant – 2 posts; project engagement under IRDL.

Age limit

Maximum 30 years; relaxation as per rules.

Important dates

Walk-in interview: 27-11-2026 at 09:30 AM.

Selection process

Verification of original documents followed by personal interview for eligible candidates.

How to apply

Attend the walk-in interview at the Seminar Room, Department of Microbiology, NEIGRIHMS, Shillong. Bring the original documents, attested photocopies and CV. No TA/DA is provided.

Documents required

Original M.Sc. qualification and experience certificates; attested photocopies of all testimonials; CV; and No Objection Certificate from the current employer, if employed.
