Serving officers who meet the circular’s analogous-post / prescribed grade-service requirements; essential qualification includes a Degree in Architecture and one year of experience in Town Planning, Civic Designing or Building Control Regulations. The notice also specifies professional-institute membership/fellowship and relevant software-course requirements.

circular की analogous-post/निर्धारित grade-service शर्तें पूरी करने वाले सेवारत अधिकारी पात्र हैं। Architecture की degree और Town Planning, Civic Designing या Building Control Regulations में एक वर्ष का अनुभव आवश्यक है। नोटिस में professional institute की membership/fellowship और संबंधित software course की शर्तें भी दी गई हैं।

One Architect post, initially two years on deputation (ISTC). Essential: Degree in Architecture and one year relevant experience in Town Planning/Civic Designing/Building Control Regulations; professional-institute membership/fellowship and software-course requirements are in the circular.

Selection Process / चयन प्रक्रिया

Applications are scrutinised through the proper channel under deputation / ISTC rules, including service record and vigilance / integrity documents.

आवेदन proper channel से deputation/ISTC नियमों के अंतर्गत जाँचे जाएँगे। service record, vigilance और integrity दस्तावेज आवश्यक हैं।

How to Apply / आवेदन कैसे करें

Obtain the prescribed bio-data form from APWD or the Andaman & Nicobar Administration website. Forward the signed application through the proper channel with vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalty statement and last five years’ ACR/APAR dossier to the Chief Engineer, APWD, Nirman Bhawan, Sri Vijaya Puram – 744101.

APWD या अंडमान-निकोबार प्रशासन की वेबसाइट से निर्धारित bio-data form लें। हस्ताक्षरित आवेदन proper channel से vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalty statement और पिछले पाँच वर्षों की ACR/APAR फाइल के साथ Chief Engineer, APWD, Nirman Bhawan, Sri Vijaya Puram – 744101 को भेजें।

Documents / आवश्यक दस्तावेज

Prescribed bio-data in duplicate; vigilance clearance; integrity certificate; statement of penalties; last five years’ ACR/APAR dossier, duly attested; qualification, experience and professional registration / membership proof.

दो प्रतियों में निर्धारित bio-data, vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalties का विवरण, पिछले पाँच वर्षों की duly attested ACR/APAR फाइल तथा qualification, experience और professional registration/membership प्रमाण।
