Recruiting organisation / भर्ती संस्था

Maharaja Bhupinder Singh Punjab Sports University, Patiala

Vacancies / पदों की संख्या

123 posts (68 Patiala + 29 Jalandhar + 26 Kala Afghana)

Location / नौकरी का स्थान

Patiala, Jalandhar and Kala Afghana (Gurdaspur), Punjab

Eligibility / योग्यता

Post-wise qualifications range from 10+2 / Diploma / Graduation to Postgraduation / Ph.D. and relevant professional qualifications. Teaching posts require discipline-specific UGC / professional qualifications and experience; non-teaching posts have separate qualification criteria.

Salary / वेतन

₹19,900 to ₹1,44,200 minimum basic pay per month, post-wise

Age limit / आयु सीमा

Teaching posts: upper age limit 45 years for General category; reserved-category relaxation as per Punjab Government rules. Non-teaching administrative / ministerial posts: 18–37 years as on 01-01-2026, subject to the detailed advertisement and applicable rules.

Application fee / आवेदन शुल्क

₹2,000 (including GST) for General category. ₹1,500 (including GST) for SC/ST/BC/EWS/ESM/PwD candidates of Punjab domicile. Separate fee is payable for each post / campus applied for. Fee is non-refundable and payable online only.

Important dates / महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ

Online application starts: 09-11-2026\nOnline application and fee deadline: 04-12-2026\nFour hard-copy sets deadline: 09-12-2026 (Registrar, MBSPSU, Patiala)\nWritten examination / Punjabi qualifying test / interview / skill test: To be notified.

Post-wise details / पद-वार विवरण

MBSPSU Patiala — 68 posts: Professor Physical Education 1; Professor Physiotherapy 2; Associate Professor Physical Education 4; Associate Professor Physiotherapy 4; Assistant Professor Physical Education 9; Assistant Professor Physiotherapy 6; Demonstrator Physiotherapy 10; Sports Trainer 4; Yoga Trainer 1; Dietician 1; University Librarian 1; Physiotherapist 6; Technical Assistant 2; Accounts Assistant 2; Office Superintendent 2; Storekeeper 1; Lab Attendant 10; Assistant Librarian 1; Clerk 1.\nGovernment Arts & Sports College, Jalandhar — 29 posts: Professor Physical Education 1; Associate Professor Physical Education 4; Assistant Professor Physical Education 9; College Librarian 1; Sports Trainer 4; Yoga Trainer 1; Dietician 1; Physiotherapist 1; Technical Assistant 2; Accounts Assistant 1; Office Superintendent 1; Storekeeper 1; Lab Attendant 2.\nGuru Nanak Government College, Kala Afghana — 26 posts: Associate Professor Physical Education 2; Assistant Professor Physical Education 6; College Librarian 1; Physiotherapist 1; Lecturer 6; Sports Trainer 4; Yoga Trainer 1; Dietician 1; Technical Assistant 1; Accounts Assistant 1; Office Superintendent 1; Storekeeper 1.\nTotal — 123 posts. Follow individual vacancy page and official advertisement for exact post-wise requirements and pay.

Selection process / चयन प्रक्रिया

Academic screening and/or written examination, Punjabi language qualifying test, skill test and/or interview as applicable to the post. Exact stage-wise criteria and merit rules are in Advertisement No. 04/2026.

Exam pattern / परीक्षा पैटर्न

No single common syllabus for all posts is stated on the careers summary. Written examination / Punjabi qualifying-test details, where applicable, are governed by the detailed Advertisement No. 04/2026 and subsequent university notices.

How to apply / आवेदन कैसे करें

1. Open the official MBSPSU Careers page.\n2. Choose the relevant post / campus under Advertisement No. 04/2026 and read its eligibility.\n3. Apply through the university recruitment application portal and pay the fee online by 04-12-2026.\n4. Print the completed application.\n5. Send four hard-copy sets (one complete set with self-attested certificates and three additional application printouts) to the Registrar, MBSPSU, Patiala by 09-12-2026.\n6. Candidates who already applied for the same posts under Advertisement No. 01/2026 need not apply again.

Documents required / आवश्यक दस्तावेज

Online application printout; one complete set with self-attested educational / professional certificates and testimonials; three additional sets of the application printout; category / domicile / EWS / ESM / PwD certificates where applicable; experience and research publications / score documents for teaching posts as prescribed; other post-specific documents in the advertisement.
