CSIR-Central Glass and Ceramic Research Institute Director Recruitment 2026

Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), CSIR-Central Glass and Ceramic Research Institute (CGCRI)

CSIR invites applications and nominations for Director, CSIR-CGCRI, Kolkata. The appointment is for six years or until superannuation, whichever is earlier.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

1 post. Ph.D. in Natural Sciences or Master’s Degree in Engineering (Ph.D. desirable for Engineering); at least 16 years’ R&D experience focused on translational research and demonstrated leadership; age 45–56 years. Refer to the official advertisement for the crucial date and relaxations.

Post-wise details

Important dates

Selection process

CSIR will assess applications and nominations through its prescribed leadership-selection process.

How to apply

Send detailed bio-data, publications / patents, research and translational contributions and a vision statement by email to drc.hqrs@csir.res.in or by post to Director Recruitment Cell, CSIR, Anusandhan Bhawan, 2 Rafi Marg, New Delhi–110001.

Documents required

Detailed bio-data; publication and patent lists; professional outputs; 1–2 page vision statement; five professional referees; qualification and experience records.
