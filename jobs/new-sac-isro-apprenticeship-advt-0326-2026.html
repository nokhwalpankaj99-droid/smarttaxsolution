Eligibility and age limit | पात्रता एवं आयु सीमा

Graduate Apprentice: relevant B.E./B.Tech or equivalent with at least 60% in the respective engineering field; B.Com, BCA and BA streams require relevant graduation with at least 60%. Technician Apprentice: relevant Engineering/Technology diploma with first class from a recognised State Board of Technical Education. Trade Apprentice: SSC/Matriculation/10th plus ITI (NTC/STC) in the relevant trade. Seat count, age and additional conditions must be checked in the PDF attached to the exact post code.

Age / आयु: 18–28 years for post codes 01–09 and 18–35 years for post codes 10–26, as on 02-12-2026. Relaxation: 3 years for OBC-NCL, 5 years for SC/ST and as per Government rules for PwBD.

Selection process | चयन प्रक्रिया

Shortlisting is based on the percentage in the qualifying Degree/Diploma/ITI examination, with reservation-category weightage. Shortlisted/empanelled candidates are called for document verification subject to availability of apprenticeship positions.

How to apply | आवेदन कैसे करें

Open the official SAC Online Recruitment portal, select Advertisement No. 0326 and the relevant post code, read its attached PDF and complete the online application by 30 November 2026.

Documents: Keep proof of age, 10th/12th/degree/diploma/ITI marks, relevant qualification certificates and identity details ready. The post-code PDF controls the exact document requirements.
