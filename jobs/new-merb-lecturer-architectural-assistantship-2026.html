Eligibility

Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent in Architecture with at least 60% marks; OR Bachelor’s Degree in Architecture plus a Master’s Degree in Architecture or an allied Architecture subject with at least 60% marks at either level. Upper age limit 32 years as on 01-11-2026; applicable relaxations as notified.
