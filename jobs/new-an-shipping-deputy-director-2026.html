Eligibility / योग्यता

Either an Indian Navy officer not below Lieutenant with sea/command experience, or an officer in Central/State/UT Government, PSU, statutory body or autonomous organisation holding an analogous post or the prescribed Level 9/8/7 service. A recognised university degree and five years’ administrative experience are essential, including three years in mainland/inter-island shipping services.

भारतीय नौसेना में Lieutenant से कम रैंक न हो और sea/command experience हो, अथवा केंद्र/राज्य/UT सरकार, PSU, statutory body या autonomous organisation में circular के अनुसार analogous post/Level 9/8/7 सेवा हो। मान्यता प्राप्त विश्वविद्यालय की degree और पाँच वर्ष का administrative experience आवश्यक है, जिसमें तीन वर्ष mainland/inter-island shipping services में होने चाहिए।

One Group-A Gazetted (Non-Ministerial) Deputy Director (Shipping Services) post, Pay Level 11, deputation for three years.

Annexure-A bio-data भरकर उम्मीदवार के हस्ताक्षर और employer/Head of Office का प्रमाणन लें। आवश्यक service records के साथ इसे Secretary (Shipping), Andaman & Nicobar Administration, Sri Vijaya Puram – 744101 को भेजें।
