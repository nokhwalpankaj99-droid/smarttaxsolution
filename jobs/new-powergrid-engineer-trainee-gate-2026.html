Eligibility – English

  • A full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc. (Engineering) degree in the relevant discipline from a recognised university or institute.
  • At least 60% marks or equivalent CGPA, subject to the detailed advertisement and applicable category concessions.
  • A qualifying GATE 2026 score in the corresponding paper: Electrical Engineering (EE), Civil Engineering (CE) or Computer Science Engineering (CS).
  • Upper age limit: 28 years as on 31 December 2025. Applicable category relaxations are governed by the official advertisement.
  • The extension notice confirms that 31 December 2025 remains the cut-off date for age, qualification and category status.

Salary and training

During the one-year training period, the stated pay scale is ₹40,000–₹1,40,000 (IDA), with basic pay of ₹40,000 plus IDA, HRA and perks at 12% of basic pay. POWERGRID gives an approximate CTC of ₹11 lakh per annum during training. After successful completion, candidates are absorbed as Engineer in E2 scale ₹50,000–₹1,60,000 (IDA), with approximate CTC of ₹22.5 lakh per annum. Actual CTC varies by posting location and applicable terms.

Selection process

  • GATE 2026 normalized marks in the corresponding paper: 85% weightage.
  • Group Discussion: 3% weightage; no qualifying marks are specified for the group discussion.
  • Personal Interview: 12% weightage; candidates must qualify the interview.
  • POWERGRID may use a psychometric assessment as an input during the interview process. Pre-employment medical fitness is required.

Important dates and fee

  • Vacancy Notice No. 2: 3 November 2026.
  • Online application opened: 3 November 2026 at 17:00 hours.
  • Original closing date: 18 November 2026 at 23:59 hours.
  • Revised closing date under Notice No. 3 dated 18 November 2026: 23 November 2026 at 23:59:59 hours.
  • Eligibility cut-off date: 31 December 2025.
  • Application fee: ₹500, non-refundable where applicable. SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen/departmental candidates are exempt as stated in the official advertisement.

How to apply

  1. Open the official POWERGRID recruitment portal using the link below.
  2. Register or log in using the GATE 2026 registration number and application number.
  3. Enter details exactly as shown on the GATE 2026 admit card and upload the required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee where applicable and submit the form before 23 November 2026 at 23:59:59 hours.
  5. Save or print the completed application and payment acknowledgement.
