Andaman PWD Senior Architect Recruitment 2026 – Deputation Vacancy

Andaman Public Works Department, Andaman & Nicobar Administration · Sri Vijaya Puram, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, India

The Andaman & Nicobar Administration has published a deputation circular for one Senior Architect in the Andaman Public Works Department. This is intended for eligible serving officers, not fresh private-sector applicants.

अंडमान एवं निकोबार प्रशासन ने Andaman Public Works Department में एक Senior Architect पद के लिए deputation circular जारी किया है। यह पात्र सेवारत सरकारी/सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र के अधिकारियों के लिए है, सामान्य fresh private-sector आवेदन नहीं।

Eligibility / योग्यता

Serving officers in Central/State/UT Government, PSU, statutory/autonomous bodies, recognised universities or research institutions who meet the circular’s analogous-post / specified grade-service conditions; essential qualifications include a Degree in Architecture, 11 years’ experience in Government/Department/PSU/Autonomous Institution and registration with the Council of Architecture.

केंद्र/राज्य/UT सरकार, PSU, statutory/autonomous संस्था, मान्यता प्राप्त विश्वविद्यालय या research institution के सेवारत अधिकारी, जो circular की analogous-post/निर्धारित grade-service शर्तें पूरी करते हों। अनिवार्य योग्यता में Architecture की degree, सरकारी/विभाग/PSU/autonomous संस्था में 11 वर्ष का अनुभव और Council of Architecture में registration शामिल है।

One Senior Architect post. Essential: Degree in Architecture; 11 years’ experience in Government organisations/departments/PSU/autonomous institution; registration with the Council of Architecture.

Salary / Pay Scale

Pay Level 13: ₹1,23,100–₹2,15,900 per month.

Important Dates / महत्वपूर्ण तिथियाँ

The Administration portal posted the circular on 19 November 2026. The notice says applications must be forwarded within 60 days from publication of the vacancy notice. 18 January 2027 is calculated from the portal posting date; the circular does not print a separate clock time.

प्रशासन के पोर्टल पर circular 19 नवम्बर 2026 को प्रकाशित हुआ। नोटिस के अनुसार आवेदन vacancy notice के प्रकाशन से 60 दिनों के भीतर भेजना है। पोर्टल की 19 नवम्बर तारीख से 18 जनवरी 2027 की गणना की गई है; circular में अलग closing time नहीं दिया गया।

The PDF states 'within 60 days from publication'. The 18 January 2027 date is calculated from the official portal posting date of 19 November 2026; the circular does not print a separate calendar date or time. Confirm any corrigendum with APWD.

Selection Process / चयन प्रक्रिया

Selection is through scrutiny of eligible officers’ service records and suitability under deputation / ISTC rules; forward the complete dossier through the proper channel.

चयन deputation/ISTC नियमों के अनुसार पात्र अधिकारियों के service record और suitability की जाँच से होगा। पूरा dossier proper channel से भेजना आवश्यक है।

How to Apply / आवेदन कैसे करें

Obtain the prescribed bio-data form from APWD / the Andaman & Nicobar Administration website. Submit signed bio-data through the proper channel with vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalty statement and the last five years’ ACR/APAR dossier to the Chief Engineer, APWD, Nirman Bhawan, Sri Vijaya Puram, Andaman & Nicobar Islands – 744101.

APWD/अंडमान-निकोबार प्रशासन की वेबसाइट से निर्धारित bio-data form लें। हस्ताक्षरित bio-data, vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalty statement और पिछले पाँच वर्षों की ACR/APAR फाइल proper channel से Chief Engineer, APWD, Nirman Bhawan, Sri Vijaya Puram, Andaman & Nicobar Islands – 744101 को भेजें।

Documents / आवश्यक दस्तावेज

Prescribed bio-data in duplicate; vigilance clearance; integrity certificate; statement of major/minor penalties; ACR/APAR dossier for the last five years, duly attested; qualification and experience records.

दो प्रतियों में निर्धारित bio-data, vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, major/minor penalties का विवरण, पिछले पाँच वर्षों की duly attested ACR/APAR फाइल तथा qualification/experience प्रमाणपत्र।
