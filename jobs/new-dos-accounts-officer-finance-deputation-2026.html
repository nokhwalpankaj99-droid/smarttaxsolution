English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Only Central Services Group-A officers in Central Government Ministries/Departments. Graduation in any discipline from a recognised Indian university/institution. Must hold analogous regular post or have five years of regular service in Pay Level 10. Financial-accounting, scrutiny of financial proposals and financial-advice experience required. Maximum age 56. Department of Space/ISRO officers are not eligible.

Post and pay

One Accounts Officer (Finance) post in Office of Joint Secretary (Finance), Department of Space Secretariat, Bengaluru. Pay Matrix Level 11.

Dates and application

Office Memorandum dated 26 October 2026 requires forwarding within 30 days; 25 November is the calculated calendar date. No closing time printed. Submit Annexure-A bio-data through proper channel with five years’ APARs, Annexure-B employer certificate, vigilance/disciplinary clearance, penalty statement and cadre NOC. Forward to Director (Personnel & Administration), Department of Space, Bengaluru.
