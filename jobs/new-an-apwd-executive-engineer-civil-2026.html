Three Executive Engineer (Civil) posts, initially two years on deputation (ISTC). Essential: Civil Engineering degree or Part A & B of the Institution of Engineers (India), plus five years’ professional experience.

How to Apply / आवेदन कैसे करें

Obtain the prescribed application form from APWD / the Andaman & Nicobar Administration website. Forward signed bio-data in duplicate through the proper channel with vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalty statement and the last five years’ ACR/APAR dossier to the Chief Engineer, APWD, Nirman Bhawan, Sri Vijaya Puram – 744101.

APWD/अंडमान-निकोबार प्रशासन की वेबसाइट से निर्धारित आवेदन फॉर्म लें। हस्ताक्षरित bio-data की दो प्रतियाँ proper channel से vigilance clearance, integrity certificate, penalty statement और पिछले पाँच वर्षों की ACR/APAR फाइल के साथ Chief Engineer, APWD, Nirman Bhawan, Sri Vijaya Puram – 744101 को भेजें।
