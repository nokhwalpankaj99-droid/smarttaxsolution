Eligibility / योग्यता

(Fire) from NFSC Nagpur; or B.E./B.Tech in Safety & Fire Engineering / Fire Technology & Safety Engineering / equivalent 4-year Fire Safety degree; or Graduate Member of Institution of Fire Engineers (India/UK); or NFSC Divisional Officers course. Post-wise details / पद-वार विवरण

Assistant Manager – Engineer (Fire), JMGS-I — 17 posts. Regular: SC 2, ST 1, OBC 1, EWS 1, UR 11 (16). Backlog: ST 1. Posting anywhere in India. Probation: 6 months.

Selection process / चयन प्रक्रिया

Online written test + Interview. Written test: English 50 questions / 50 marks / 40 minutes; Professional Knowledge 75 questions / 75 marks / 80 minutes; total 125 questions / 125 marks / 2 hours. No negative marking. Interview carries 100 marks. Final normalized weightage: written 70, interview 30. SBI reserves the right to revise the selection mode.

Exam pattern / परीक्षा पैटर्न

English Language and Professional Knowledge. Professional Knowledge covers Fire Engineering, Fire Service Administration and Fire Service Regulatory Acts / By-laws. Role requires knowledge of fire pumps, hydrant systems, detection and alarm systems, sprinklers, building evacuation and fire safety audits.
