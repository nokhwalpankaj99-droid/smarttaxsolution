Department of Space Group A & B Deputation Recruitment 2026 – 60 Posts

Government of India – Department of Space Secretariat, Bengaluru

Department of Space has extended the deadline for 60 Group A and Group B deputation posts across 16 designations in Bengaluru to 30 November 2026. Pay Matrix Levels 7–13. Applications must be forwarded through proper channel by eligible serving officers.

Posts60 posts across 16 designations
LocationDepartment of Space Secretariat, Antariksh Bhavan, New BEL Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560094, India
Employment typeDeputation for serving government officers; post-wise tenure generally three to five years
Salary / stipend7th CPC Pay Matrix Levels 7–13, depending on post
Last date30 November 2026
NoticeA.26011/1/2025-I (Vol.II); extension dated 6 November 2026

Deadline note: 30 November 2026; no closing time stated.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

Deputation-only, not fresh direct recruitment. Eligible serving officers of ISRO, All India Services and Central Services under Central Government, as specified by post. Must hold analogous regular post or required regular service in feeder pay level/cadre. Maximum age 56 years.

Post-wise vacancies

Group A – 32: Sr. Head Audit (1); Principal Pay & Accounts Officer (1); Deputy Secretary (2); Under Secretary (11); Sr. Audit Officer (4); Principal Accounts Officer (1); Audit Officer (5); Accounts Officer (1); Principal Staff Officer (1); Senior Principal Private Secretary (1); Principal Private Secretary (3); Assistant Director (Official Language) (1).

Group B – 28: Assistant Section Officer (17); Private Secretary (6); Personal Assistant (3); Senior Translation Officer (2).

Pay and selection

7th CPC Pay Matrix Levels 7–13. Departmental Selection Committee evaluates service records and past performance; panel valid one year.

Dates and application

Extension memorandum dated 6 November 2026 sets last date at 30 November 2026. Complete Annexure-A/B, attach APARs for preceding five years, vigilance/disciplinary clearance, penalty statement and cadre NOC. Forward through proper channel to Director (Personnel & Administration), Department of Space, Antariksh Bhavan, New BEL Road, Bengaluru 560094.
