AIIMS Deoghar Contractual Non-Faculty Recruitment 2026 – 4 Posts

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Deoghar – Recruitment Cell

AIIMS Deoghar invites offline applications for four contractual non-faculty posts: Security Officer, Biomedical Engineer, Laundry Manager and Perfusionist. The notice sets the deadline at 30 days from publication in Employment News but does not print a calendar date.

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

  • Security Officer: degree plus serving Police/Armed/Paramilitary officer with analogous post or prescribed regular service; security experience preferred.
  • Biomedical Engineer: B.E./B.Tech Biomedical or diploma plus two years’ relevant experience.
  • Laundry Manager: 12th, diploma/certificate in Dry Cleaning/Laundry Technology and 12 years’ mechanised-laundry experience.
  • Perfusionist: B.Sc., recognised Perfusion Technology certificate and at least one year clinical perfusion experience.

Posts, pay and tenure

One post each; all unreserved. Monthly consolidated pay: Security Officer ₹91,443; Biomedical Engineer ₹73,187; Laundry Manager ₹57,702; Perfusionist ₹57,702. Contract 11 months or until regular appointment, whichever earlier.

Dates and selection

Advertisement dated 18 November 2026. Last date: 30 days from Employment News publication; issue date and calendar deadline are not printed. Shortlisted candidates will be emailed about document verification and walk-in interview.

How to apply

Use the prescribed form in the PDF; attach self-attested documents; demand draft fee: General ₹3,000, OBC ₹1,000, SC/ST/PwD/Women nil. Send by post to Recruitment Cell, AIIMS Deoghar, Devipur Campus, Ramsagar, Deoghar-814117, Jharkhand. Government employees attach NOC.
