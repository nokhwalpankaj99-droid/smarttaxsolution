NIDM Associate Professor Recruitment 2026 – 2 Deputation Posts

National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

NIDM has extended the deadline for two Associate Professor (Group A) posts at its Vijayawada Campus to 30 November 2026.

Total vacancies2 posts
LocationNIDM Vijayawada Campus, Kondapavuluru Village, Gannavaram Mandal, Krishna District, Andhra Pradesh, India
Employment typeGroup A – Deputation including Short-Term Contract
Salary / Pay7th CPC Pay Level 12
Last date2026-11-30
AdvertisementNIDM/Admin/rect. on deputation/327/2022 (part file)

English – Vacancy details

Eligibility

2 posts. Master’s in Social Sciences, Environmental / Earth Science, Engineering, Disaster Management or allied subject with at least 55%, plus Ph.D. in the concerned / allied discipline; at least 10 years’ teaching or research experience in a regular position equivalent to Assistant Professor and at least five qualifying publications. Deputation applicants must meet parent-cadre and service conditions; maximum age 56 years.

Post-wise details

Associate Professor (Group A) – 2 posts at NIDM Vijayawada Campus; Pay Level 12.

Age limit

Maximum 56 years for deputation.

Important dates

Extended last date: 30-11-2026. No closing time stated.

Selection process

Deputation / short-term contract selection after scrutiny of sponsored applications and service records.

How to apply

Submit the prescribed application in duplicate through the parent Ministry / Department / State Government / UT administration or eligible organisation, duly countersigned. Include APARs and vigilance / integrity certificates. Send to the Executive Director, NIDM, Ministry of Home Affairs, Plot 15, Pocket 3, Block B, Sector 29, Rohini, Delhi–110042. Advance copy may be emailed to recruitment.nidm@nic.in.

Documents required

Prescribed application; APARs / ACRs for five years; integrity and vigilance certificates; qualification and service records; competent-authority sponsorship.
