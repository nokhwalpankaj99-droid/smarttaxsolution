Supreme Court Junior Court Assistant Answer Key 2026

The Supreme Court Recruitment Cell notice dated 22 November 2026 confirms that answer keys for the Junior Court Assistant Objective Type Test held on 20 November 2026 have been uploaded. The notice provides an online link for candidates to submit objections until 11:55 PM on 23 November 2026.

English – What candidates should do

  1. Open the official candidate portal using the link below.
  2. Sign in with the credentials used for the Junior Court Assistant recruitment, as requested by the portal.
  3. Review the answer key and question details carefully.
  4. If an objection is permitted, submit it through the official portal before 11:55 PM on 23 November 2026 and retain the acknowledgement.

The public notice does not reproduce the candidate-specific answer key in its text; the official candidate portal is the access point.

हिंदी – उम्मीदवारों के लिए जानकारी

  1. नीचे दिए गए आधिकारिक उम्मीदवार पोर्टल को खोलें।
  2. Junior Court Assistant भर्ती के आवेदन में उपयोग किए गए credentials से लॉगिन करें।
  3. अपनी Answer Key और प्रश्नों की जानकारी ध्यान से जाँचें।
  4. यदि किसी उत्तर पर आपत्ति करनी हो, तो 23 नवम्बर 2026 रात 11:55 बजे से पहले केवल आधिकारिक पोर्टल पर submit करें और acknowledgement सुरक्षित रखें।
