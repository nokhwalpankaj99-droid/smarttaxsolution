Update Summary / अपडेट का सार

SSC has uploaded tentative answer keys and response sheets for five Junior Engineer Examination, 2026 papers: Civil & Structural, Electrical, Mechanical, Computer Science & Information Technology, and Physics. Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering is excluded from this upload.

SSC ने Junior Engineer Examination, 2026 के पाँच papers—Civil & Structural, Electrical, Mechanical, Computer Science & Information Technology और Physics—की tentative answer keys और response sheets अपलोड की हैं। Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering इस upload में शामिल नहीं है।

What candidates should do / उम्मीदवार क्या करें

Log in to SSC with Registration Number and Password. Review the response sheet and raise challenges online only during the stated window. The fee is ₹50 per question/answer challenged. Challenges submitted after 22 November 2026 at 6:00 PM or through another mode will not be accepted.

Registration Number और Password से SSC में login करें। Response sheet जाँचें और निर्धारित समय में केवल online challenge दर्ज करें। प्रति प्रश्न/उत्तर challenge का शुल्क ₹50 है। 22 नवम्बर 2026 शाम 6 बजे के बाद या अन्य माध्यम से भेजी गई आपत्तियाँ स्वीकार नहीं होंगी।
