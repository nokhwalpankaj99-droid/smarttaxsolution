Shortlist / Result Update

India Post GDS Schedule-II July 2026 – List-I of Shortlisted Candidates

Authority: Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications, Government of India

CoverageAll India
Update typeList-I of shortlisted candidates
Release dateNot displayed on the official portal page
Exam dateNo examination date is stated for this shortlist update
Current statusOfficial GDS portal displays “List-I of Shortlisted Candidates Published”
Application statusRegistration and application submission for Schedule-II, July-2026 are closed

The official India Post GDS Online Engagement portal currently displays a notice that List-I of shortlisted candidates for Schedule-II, July-2026 has been published. The accessible portal text does not show the publication date, candidate count or a direct list file. Candidates should open the official portal and use the “Shortlisted Candidates: Schedule-II, July-2026” section.

English – How to check

  1. Open the official India Post GDS Online Engagement page.
  2. Go to the “Shortlisted Candidates: Schedule-II, July-2026” section.
  3. Open the relevant list or circle entry available on the official portal and verify your registration details carefully.
  4. If the portal asks for candidate authentication, use the official Candidate Login page only.

The official portal states that registration for Schedule-II, July-2026 closed on 19 September 2026 at 17:00 hours and application submission closed on 21 September 2026 at 17:00 hours. This is a shortlist update, not a fresh application window.

हिंदी – सूची कैसे देखें

  1. India Post के आधिकारिक GDS Online Engagement पोर्टल को खोलें।
  2. “Shortlisted Candidates: Schedule-II, July-2026” सेक्शन में जाएँ।
  3. आधिकारिक पोर्टल पर उपलब्ध संबंधित सूची/सर्किल entry खोलकर अपने registration विवरण जाँचें।
  4. यदि लॉगिन माँगा जाए, तो केवल आधिकारिक Candidate Login पेज का उपयोग करें।

आधिकारिक पोर्टल के अनुसार Schedule-II, July-2026 के लिए registration 19 सितम्बर 2026 को शाम 5 बजे और application submission 21 सितम्बर 2026 को शाम 5 बजे बंद हो गया था। यह shortlist/result update है, नया आवेदन आमंत्रण नहीं।

उपलब्ध official page के text में List-I की publication date, shortlisted उम्मीदवारों की संख्या या direct PDF link प्रदर्शित नहीं है। सही जानकारी के लिए official portal के Shortlisted Candidates सेक्शन को देखें।
