TNPSC Combined Technical Services Diploma / ITI Level Hall Ticket 2026

Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC)

Combined Technical Services Examination (Diploma / ITI Level) – Notification No. 06/2026 and Addendum No. 6A/2026 · Released: 02 October 2026

Update summary | अपडेट का सार

TNPSC’s official press release dated 02 October 2026 states that the Memorandum of Admission (Hall Ticket) for the Combined Technical Services Examination (Diploma / ITI Level), Notification No. 06/2026 with Addendum No. 6A/2026, has been hosted on the Commission’s websites. The published schedule includes a 22–24 November 2026 examination block. Candidates can download the hall ticket through the One Time Registration (OTR) Dashboard using their Application Number and Date of Birth. The individual hall ticket controls the candidate’s exact date, session and venue.

TNPSC की 02 अक्टूबर 2026 की आधिकारिक प्रेस विज्ञप्ति के अनुसार Combined Technical Services Examination (Diploma / ITI Level), Notification No. 06/2026 और Addendum No. 6A/2026 का Memorandum of Admission आयोग की वेबसाइटों पर उपलब्ध कराया गया है। प्रकाशित कार्यक्रम में 22–24 नवम्बर 2026 का परीक्षा चरण शामिल है। उम्मीदवार Application Number और Date of Birth से OTR Dashboard में लॉगिन करके Hall Ticket डाउनलोड कर सकते हैं। व्यक्तिगत Hall Ticket पर दी गई तिथि, session और केंद्र अंतिम होंगे।
