English – Official update

What was released

OPSC’s official “What’s New” page lists a Geologist-2025 Written Examination notice for objections to questions and answers, released on 20 November 2026. This is an objection-window update, not a declaration of result or final merit list.

Exam date and status

The exam date is not stated in the notice listing. The official page shows the objection-submission link as available through 30 November 2026. Candidates should open the specific Geologist-2025 row on OPSC’s official page and follow the linked PDF and submission instructions.

How to check / submit

  1. Open the official OPSC What’s New page.
  2. Find the entry for “Written Examination for Geologist-2025”.
  3. Open the official notice and the linked objection-submission facility.
  4. Submit any permitted objection within the official window and retain the acknowledgement.
