Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Smart Tax Solution (“we”, “our” or “us”) respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us for services. This policy describes how information may be handled when you use this website.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp or the enquiry form, you may provide your name, contact details, service enquiry and information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive personal information through the website or ordinary messaging.

How information is used

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, discuss services, communicate with you and maintain relevant business records. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Third-party services and links

This website links to external government portals and may open WhatsApp, email or telephone applications. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their websites or data practices.

Security and retention

We take reasonable care when handling information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain enquiry information only as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, legal obligations or legitimate recordkeeping.

Your choices and contact

You may contact us to ask about information you have provided or request correction where appropriate. For privacy-related queries, email smarttaxsolutionsgnr@gmail.com or call 96640-29638.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when our website or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

This is a general website privacy notice and may need review for your specific business practices and applicable legal requirements.
